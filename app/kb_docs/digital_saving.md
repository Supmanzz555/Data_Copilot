# Digital Saving Product Documentation

## Overview
dBank Digital Saving is a high-yield savings account accessible entirely through our mobile app, offering competitive interest rates and instant access to funds.

## Product Features
- **Interest Rate**: 2.5% p.a. (up to 3.5% for balances >100,000 THB)
- **Minimum Balance**: 100 THB (no maintenance fee)
- **Withdrawal Limit**: 50,000 THB per day via app
- **Interest Calculation**: Daily, credited monthly on 1st
- **Account Opening**: Instant via app (full KYC)

## Interest Rate Tiers
| Balance (THB) | Interest Rate p.a. |
|---------------|-------------------|
| 100 - 9,999 | 2.0% |
| 10,000 - 49,999 | 2.5% |
| 50,000 - 99,999 | 3.0% |
| 100,000+ | 3.5% |

## Features
- **Auto-Sweep**: Automatically moves excess funds from checking account
- **Goal Setting**: Create multiple savings goals with auto-transfer
- **Round-Up**: Automatically save spare change from transactions
- **Withdrawal**: Instant via app, free unlimited withdrawals

## How to Open Account
1. Login to dBank mobile app
2. Tap "Open Digital Saving"
3. Complete video KYC verification
4. Set initial deposit amount (minimum 100 THB)
5. Account activated instantly
6. Transfer funds from linked account

## Deposit Methods
- Transfer from linked dBank account (instant, free)
- Transfer from other Thai banks (1-2 hours, free)
- Cash deposit at dBank branches (instant)
- PromptPay QR code deposit

## Withdrawal Methods
- Transfer to linked account (instant, free)
- Transfer to other banks (2-24 hours, free)
- ATM withdrawal (standard ATM fees apply)
- Counter withdrawal at branches

## Known Issues (As of v1.2)
- **High Priority**: Interest calculation occasionally delays by 1 day (affects ~2% of accounts on month-end)
- **Medium**: Deposit confirmation email may not send immediately (SMS works fine)
- **Low**: In-app interest calculator shows incorrect projections for amounts >1M THB

## Troubleshooting
**Q: Interest not credited on 1st of month?**
A: Check account statement. If still missing after 2 days, contact support.

**Q: Withdrawal failed?**
A: Ensure daily limit (50K) not exceeded. Check beneficiary account details.

**Q: Auto-sweep not working?**
A: Verify auto-sweep is enabled in Settings > Auto-Sweep. Minimum balance for sweep: 1,000 THB.

## Policies
- **Dormant Account**: Account with no transactions for 12 months flagged as dormant
- **Account Closure**: Can close anytime via app or branch. Funds transferred immediately.
- **Tax**: Interest income subject to 15% withholding tax (auto-deducted)
- **Deposit Insurance**: Protected up to 1,000,000 THB per depositor by DPA (Deposit Protection Agency)
