# Digital Lending Product Documentation

## Overview
dBank Digital Lending provides instant personal loans through our mobile app, serving Thai customers with quick approval and flexible repayment options.

## Product Features
- **Loan Amount**: 5,000 - 500,000 THB
- **Interest Rate**: Starting from 12% p.a. (varies by credit score)
- **Tenure**: 3, 6, 12, 24, or 36 months
- **Approval Time**: Typically within 2-24 hours
- **Disbursement**: Direct to linked bank account within 1 hour of approval

## Eligibility Criteria
- Thai citizen or permanent resident
- Age: 20-65 years
- Minimum monthly income: 15,000 THB
- Valid Thai national ID
- dBank account active for at least 3 months
- Good credit score (minimum 600)

## Application Process
1. Open dBank mobile app
2. Navigate to "Digital Lending"
3. Enter desired loan amount and tenure
4. Upload required documents (ID, income proof, bank statement)
5. E-sign loan agreement
6. Await approval notification
7. Funds disbursed upon approval

## Required Documents
- Thai National ID card (both sides)
- Latest 3 months bank statements
- Employment letter or recent payslip
- House registration (Tabien Baan) copy

## Interest Calculation
- Interest calculated on reducing balance method
- No hidden fees
- Early repayment allowed without penalty
- Late payment fee: 500 THB + 18% p.a. on overdue amount

## Repayment Options
- Auto-debit from dBank savings account (recommended)
- Manual payment via mobile app
- Bank transfer to designated account
- 7-Eleven counter payment (fee applies)

## Known Issues (As of v1.2)
- **High Priority**: Loan approval notifications delayed during peak hours (10am-2pm)
- **Medium**: EMI schedule may take up to 5 minutes to update after disbursement
- **Low**: PDF statement download occasionally fails on iOS 16.4

## Troubleshooting
**Q: Application stuck at "Processing"?**
A: Wait 24 hours. If no response, contact support at 02-xxx-xxxx

**Q: Disbursement not received?**
A: Check linked bank account details. Funds typically arrive within 1 hour, max 24 hours.

**Q: Cannot upload documents?**
A: Ensure file size <5MB, format: JPG/PNG/PDF only. Clear app cache and retry.

## Policies & Compliance
- Regulated by Bank of Thailand (BoT)
- Licensed under Thai Commercial Banking Act
- Interest rate capped as per BoT regulations
- Customer data protected under PDPA (Personal Data Protection Act)
- Maximum loan-to-income ratio: 5x monthly salary
