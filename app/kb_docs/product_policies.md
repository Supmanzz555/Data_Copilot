# Product Policies - dBank Thailand

**Effective Date**: January 1, 2025  
**Last Revised**: January 15, 2025  
**Regulatory Body**: Bank of Thailand (BoT)

## General Banking Policies

### Account Eligibility
- **Minimum Age**: 20 years (18 with parental consent)
- **Citizenship**: Thai citizen or permanent resident with valid documentation
- **Know Your Customer (KYC)**: All customers must complete video KYC verification
- **Politically Exposed Persons (PEP)**: Enhanced due diligence required

### Account Minimums & Limits
| Product | Minimum Balance | Monthly Fee if Below | Transaction Limit |
|---------|-----------------|---------------------|-------------------|
| Digital Saving | 100 THB | None | 50,000 THB/day withdrawal |
| Checking Account | 500 THB | 50 THB/month | 100,000 THB/day |
| Digital Lending | N/A | N/A | 500,000 THB max loan |
| Investment | 1,000 THB | None | No daily limit |

## Digital Saving Policies

### Interest Rates (Tiered)
- Updated quarterly based on BoT policy rate
- Current rates (Q1 2025): 2.0% - 3.5% p.a.
- Interest compounded daily, credited monthly
- Subject to 15% withholding tax (auto-deducted)

### Withdrawal Policies
- **Daily Limit**: 50,000 THB via app
- **Monthly Limit**: No limit
- **Branch Withdrawals**: Up to 200,000 THB/day with ID
- **Emergency Withdrawals**: Contact support for higher limits (verification required)

### Fees
- **Account Maintenance**: Free
- **Withdrawals**: Free (unlimited via app)
- **ATM Withdrawals**: Free at dBank ATMs, 20 THB at other banks (charged by other bank)
- **International Transfers**: As per BoT exchange rate + 200 THB fee

### Dormant Account Policy
- Account inactive for 12 months flagged as dormant
- No fees charged on dormant accounts
- Reactivation: Login to app or visit branch

## Digital Lending Policies

### Loan Eligibility
- **Age**: 20-65 years
- **Income**: Minimum 15,000 THB/month
- **Employment**: Employed/self-employed for min 6 months
- **Credit Score**: Minimum 600 (dBank internal score)
- **dBank Account**: Active for minimum 3 months
- **Existing Loans**: Max 3 active loans (any lender) allowed

### Loan Terms
- **Amount**: 5,000 - 500,000 THB
- **Interest**: 12% - 28% p.a. (risk-based pricing)
- **Tenure**: 3, 6, 12, 24, or 36 months
- **Processing Fee**: 2% of loan amount (min 500 THB, max 5,000 THB)
- **Prepayment**: Allowed anytime, no penalty

### Repayment Terms
- **EMI Due Date**: Same date each month as loan disbursement
- **Grace Period**: 3 days after due date
- **Late Payment Fee**: 500 THB + 18% p.a. on overdue amount
- **Repayment Methods**: Auto-debit (recommended), manual payment via app, bank transfer

### Default Policy
- **30 Days Overdue**: Account marked as delinquent, reported to credit bureau
- **60 Days Overdue**: Legal notice sent
- **90 Days Overdue**: Loan referred to collection agency
- **Impact**: Credit score severely affected, may impact future loan eligibility

### Loan-to-Income Ratio
- Maximum 5x monthly salary (as per BoT guidelines)
- Example: 20,000 THB/month income = max 100,000 THB loan

## Age-Related Policies

### Minors (Under 18)
- Cannot open accounts independently
- Require parental/guardian consent and joint account
- Limited transaction capabilities
- Full access granted upon turning 18

### Young Adults (18-20)
- Can open Digital Saving with parental consent
- Cannot apply for loans
- Investment products require financial literacy assessment

### Senior Citizens (65+)
- Can hold Digital Saving accounts
- Digital Lending limited (max tenure: to age 70)
- Enhanced support available (dedicated hotline)
- Branch visit assistance for complex transactions

## Regulatory Compliance

### Data Protection (PDPA)
- Customer data encrypted at rest and in transit
- Data retention: 7 years post-account closure (regulatory requirement)
- Data portability: Customers can request data export
- Right to be forgotten: After 7-year retention period

### Anti-Money Laundering (AML)
- Transactions >50,000 THB require additional verification
- Suspicious transactions auto-flagged and reported to AMLO
- Enhanced due diligence for high-risk customers
- Employee training: Quarterly AML updates

### Deposit Protection
- Deposits protected up to 1,000,000 THB per depositor
- Protected by Deposit Protection Agency (DPA)
- Covers principal + accrued interest
- Multiple accounts aggregated for protection limit

## Account Closure Policies

### Voluntary Closure
- Can close anytime via app or branch
- All outstanding loans must be settled first
- Remaining balance transferred to nominated account immediately
- Account data retained for 7 years (regulatory requirement)

### Involuntary Closure
- Fraudulent activity: Immediate closure, reported to authorities
- Consistent negative balance (checking): 90 days notice
- Non-compliance with KYC: 30 days to comply or account frozen

---

## Dispute Resolution

### Transaction Disputes
- Report within 30 days of transaction
- Investigation completed within 15 business days
- Provisional credit if dispute valid
- Permanent reversal after merchant confirmation

### Complaint Escalation
1. In-app support (response within 24 hours)
2. Email to complaints@dbank.co.th (response within 48 hours)
3. Escalation to Compliance Officer (within 7 days)
4. External: BoT Financial Consumer Protection Center

## Fee Schedule (Effective Q1 2025)

### Account Fees
- Account opening: Free
- Account maintenance: Free (if minimum balance maintained)
- Monthly statement: Free (digital), 50 THB (paper by mail)
- Duplicate statements: 100 THB per statement

### Transaction Fees
- Internal transfers: Free
- Other Thai bank transfers: Free (via PromptPay), 25 THB (SWIFT)
- International transfers: 200 THB + correspondent bank fees
- Cash deposit: Free at branches, 0.5% at non-dBank locations

### Card Fees
- ATM card: Free
- Replacement card: 100 THB
- International usage: 2.5% of transaction + 150 THB per transaction
